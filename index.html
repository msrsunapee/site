About us

Main Street Retreat in Sunapee is a family-friendly vacation home with plenty of room to spread out. The house has four bedrooms, two bathrooms, and two living rooms, plus an extra game room. Outside, you can relax on the deck, soak in the saltwater hot tub, or gather around the Mexican-style fireplace in the private yard.
